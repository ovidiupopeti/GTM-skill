# OutThink ICP and messaging reference

Use this reference as a disclosed qualification hypothesis derived from OutThink's public website on 2026-08-28. It is not a model of proven win probability. Revisit it when CRM, win/loss, or customer-interview evidence becomes available.

## Product and category

OutThink describes itself as an AI Human Risk Management platform for enterprises. Its core promise is to move security programs from compliance activity and generic phishing metrics toward secure behavior and measurable human-risk reduction. The platform's four recurring jobs are Motivate, Educate, Activate, and Correct.

## Five-dimension scorecard

Score each dimension `0 = absent/contrary`, `1 = plausible but incomplete`, or `2 = strong cited evidence`.

1. **Workforce scale and complexity:** large, distributed, multilingual, frontline, multi-entity, or multi-country workforce. Customer examples range from about 1,300 to 280,000 employees; enterprise capabilities deepen above 2,000 users.
2. **Human-risk exposure:** regulated sector, critical infrastructure, OT, financial transactions, sensitive data, or an attack surface where employee behavior matters.
3. **Program signal:** public evidence of security awareness, phishing simulation, role-based training, security culture, or a named human-risk initiative.
4. **Change trigger or gap:** AI-enabled threats, plateaued engagement, compliance-only metrics, manual program work, fragmented tooling, integration/reporting complexity, or pressure to quantify risk.
5. **OutThink operating fit:** need for adaptive content, behavior-based intervention, global orchestration, security-stack integration, measurable risk, or reduced administration.

Do not award `2` for company size alone. Do not turn absence of public evidence into a negative fact.

## Likely buying committee

- Primary: CISO; Head/Director of Security Awareness, Human Risk, Security Culture, or Cyber Education.
- Supporting: GRC or Security Compliance leader; SOC/Security Operations; IAM; HR/L&D where governance permits.
- Potential blocker: privacy, legal, employee relations, procurement, or an incumbent platform owner.

Use roles only. Never identify or profile individuals for the public Skillathon artifact.

## Message pillars

Choose only pillars supported by target-company evidence:

- Move from completion and click metrics to observable secure behavior and measurable risk reduction.
- Adapt training and interventions to role, risk, context, and behavior instead of sending everyone the same content.
- Prepare people for AI-enabled phishing, deepfakes, vishing, smishing, and risks beyond phishing.
- Connect human-risk intelligence to the wider security stack and operational decisions.
- Reduce manual campaign, segmentation, content, reporting, and follow-up work.
- Start at the organization's current HRM maturity level and progress without re-platforming.

## Outreach quality bar

The opening observation must be specific enough that it could not be sent unchanged to every enterprise, yet must be supported by a company-owned source. Ask a diagnostic question rather than asserting a hidden pain. Use one OutThink capability as the bridge. Treat the copy as a hypothesis to test and track replies and objections before declaring it effective.

## Boundaries

- Company communications tone is observable; an individual's personality is not.
- Public evidence can justify relevance, not intent to buy.
- Do not imply that OutThink monitored employees or inspected personal activity.
- Do not use scraped profiles, inferred traits, contact details, or personal social engagement.
- Do not promise a reply, risk reduction percentage, or business outcome without directly applicable evidence.
